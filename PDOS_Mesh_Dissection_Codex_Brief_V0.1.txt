PDoS / Codex Rhino reconstruction experiment — V0.1

OBJECTIVE
Use the STL as geometric truth and the JSON as a dissection/curve package. The goal is NOT to convert every mesh triangle to NURBS. The goal is to reconstruct a small number of editable curves/surfaces while preserving the generated shape.

FILES
- QiQeHV_-aQnoxgHyISf3L.stl
- PDOS_Mesh_Dissection_V0.1.json

PHASE 1 — REFERENCE
1. Import STL unchanged to layer PDOS_REF_MESH and lock it.
2. Preserve native STL coordinates and scale. Units are unknown; do not guess mm yet.
3. Separate the 2 connected components. Reconstruct component 0 first; component 1 is a small detached detail.

PHASE 2 — CURVE CAGE
4. Read sections.x / sections.y / sections.z from JSON.
5. Create each simplified polyline on the matching layer PDOS_SEC_X/Y/Z.
6. For each meaningful closed loop, fit a degree-3 periodic NURBS curve. Use a conservative fit tolerance around 0.0015 × bbox diagonal.
7. Keep BOTH raw simplified polylines and fitted NURBS curves for comparison.
8. Do not build surfaces yet. First save screenshots of the 3D curve cage overlaid on the locked reference mesh.

PHASE 3 — REGION INTERPRETATION
9. Identify where loop topology remains stable across neighboring stations. Those ranges are candidates for one coherent surface patch.
10. Do not loft through stations where loop count/topology changes. Split the product into regions there.
11. Use the X/Y/Z section families together to decide surface flow. Prefer Sweep2/NetworkSrf/RebuildUV over blind global Loft when cross-direction constraints are needed.

PHASE 4 — SURFACE REBUILD
12. Rebuild only one dominant visible region first as a test patch.
13. Target low CV count. Rebuild curves before increasing surface complexity.
14. Use MatchSrf for G1/G2 where design intent suggests smooth continuation.
15. Compare rebuilt surface against reference mesh by sampled closest-point deviation. Record max and RMS deviation.

HARD RULES
- Do not use MeshToNURB as the final reconstruction strategy.
- Do not convert all 506,186 triangles into individual NURBS faces.
- Do not invent hidden geometry when the mesh already supplies XYZ truth.
- Do not force all sections into one loft.
- Keep construction curves editable and on separate layers.

FIRST DELIVERABLE
A Rhino file containing: locked reference mesh + reconstructed X/Y/Z NURBS section cage only. No final surfaces yet. This lets us judge whether the geometric dissection preserves the Hunyuan model before committing to surface topology.
